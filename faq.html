Booking & Reservations

You can book a tour by visiting our booking page and filling out the tour booking form. Select your preferred tour package, choose your desired dates, and provide your contact information. Alternatively, you can contact us directly at +94 77 123 4567 or email us at ecoxploresl@gmail.com.

Most of our tours can accommodate groups of 2 or more people. However, individual bookings are also welcome. We recommend a minimum of 2-4 people for the best experience. For large groups (10+), please contact us directly for special arrangements and potential group discounts.

We accept multiple payment methods including credit/debit cards, bank transfers, and online payment platforms. A 30% advance payment is required to confirm your booking, with the remaining balance due 7 days before the tour date. We also offer flexible payment plans for larger groups.

Yes, you can cancel or reschedule your booking up to 14 days before the tour date with a full refund (minus any non-refundable fees). Cancellations made 7-14 days before the tour will receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 7 days of the tour are non-refundable. For rescheduling, we're happy to accommodate you based on availability.

Tour Information & Details

Each tour package includes experienced guides, meals (breakfast, lunch, and dinner), safety equipment, and accommodation where applicable. Specific inclusions vary by tour:

  • Mountain Hiking: Guide, meals, camping gear, maps
  • River Rafting: Safety gear, guide, meals, all equipment
  • Eco Camping: Tent, guide, meals, bonfire
  • Mountain Cycling: Bike, helmet, guide, meals, repair kit
  • Jungle Safari: Safari vehicle, guide, meals, accommodation
  • Coastal Retreat: Snorkel gear, boat, guide, meals, beach resort stay

Our tours cater to different fitness levels. Mountain Hiking and Mountain Cycling require moderate fitness. River Rafting and Jungle Safari are suitable for most fitness levels. Eco Camping and Coastal Retreat are relaxed experiences. If you have specific concerns about your fitness level, please contact us and we can recommend suitable tours or provide modifications.

Essential items to bring:

  • Comfortable, weather-appropriate clothing
  • Sturdy hiking boots or sports shoes
  • Personal toiletries and medications
  • Reusable water bottle (we encourage eco-friendly practices)
  • Sunscreen and insect repellent
  • Camera or smartphone for photos
  • Light backpack for daily essentials
  • Any required personal items
Specific packing lists are provided after booking confirmation.

Tour durations vary:

  • Mountain Hiking Adventure: 3 Days / 2 Nights
  • River Rafting Experience: 2 Days / 1 Night
  • Eco Camping Experience: 2 Days / 1 Night
  • Mountain Cycling Adventure: 4 Days / 3 Nights
  • Jungle Safari & Wildlife Tour: 3 Days / 2 Nights
  • Coastal Retreat & Snorkeling: 5 Days / 4 Nights
Custom duration packages are available upon request.

Eco-Friendly & Sustainability

Absolutely! All EcoXplore Adventures tours are designed with sustainability and environmental conservation in mind. We follow strict eco-friendly practices including:

  • Minimizing waste through reusable equipment and packaging
  • Supporting local communities and eco-tourism projects
  • Using sustainable transportation methods
  • Training guides in environmental conservation
  • Protecting wildlife habitats and ecosystems
We contribute a portion of our profits to local conservation projects.

We implement the "Leave No Trace" principle on all tours. Our guides ensure participants:

  • Don't leave any waste behind
  • Stay on designated trails
  • Respect wildlife and maintain safe distances
  • Use biodegradable products only
  • Minimize noise and disturbances
We regularly conduct environmental audits and work with conservation organizations.

Safety & Health

Basic accident and liability insurance is included in all tour packages. However, we strongly recommend that participants also obtain comprehensive travel insurance that covers personal belongings, medical emergencies, and trip cancellations. Your insurance should ideally include coverage for adventure activities. We can provide recommendations for insurance providers if needed.

All our guides are trained in first aid and CPR. We carry comprehensive first aid kits and have established protocols for medical emergencies. Our tours operate in areas with accessible medical facilities, and we maintain communication with local emergency services. Before departure, we collect medical information to ensure appropriate support. Participants with serious medical conditions should inform us during booking.

Health requirements depend on your location and the specific tour region. We recommend:

  • Consulting your healthcare provider 4-6 weeks before the tour
  • Carrying necessary personal medications
  • Informing us of any allergies or health conditions
  • Following local health advisories
Contact us before booking if you have specific health concerns.

Most tours are suitable for participants aged 10-70. Specific restrictions:

  • Mountain Hiking & Cycling: Ages 12+
  • River Rafting: Ages 10+ (with parental consent for under 16)
  • Jungle Safari: All ages welcome
  • Coastal Retreat: All ages welcome
  • Eco Camping: Ages 8+ (family-friendly)
Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult. Senior travelers (60+) should have good health. Contact us to discuss your specific situation.

Weather & Conditions

Our guides monitor weather conditions closely. For minor weather issues, tours proceed with appropriate precautions. For severe weather (heavy storms, flooding, etc.), we may:

  • Modify the tour itinerary to safer alternatives
  • Reschedule for another date
  • Offer a full refund if rescheduling isn't possible
Your safety is our highest priority. We reserve the right to make changes for your protection.

Optimal seasons for each tour:

  • Mountain Hiking: December to March (dry season)
  • River Rafting: July to September (good water flow)
  • Eco Camping: Year-round (varies by location)
  • Mountain Cycling: November to February
  • Jungle Safari: June to September (wildlife active)
  • Coastal Retreat: April to September (warm, dry weather)
Tours are available year-round, but seasonal variations affect experiences. Contact us for recommendations based on your preferences.
